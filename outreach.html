🎨 Paint a Cilium

Take a relaxing moment. You'll see microscopy images of primary cilia — tiny antenna-like organelles on cells. Cilia are visible in red; the basal body — the structure anchoring the cilium to the cell — is yellow; the cell nucleus is blue.

Do scientists really do this? Yes, they do! Many AI models to detect subcellular structures have been trained by scientists marking structures in real microscopy images.

How to play:

  1. Pick a color and paint over one cilium.
  2. Use a different color for each separate cilium.
  3. Golden rule: never give two touching cilia the same color.
  4. Feedback: At the top of the image, our app gives you feedback on whether you hit a signal-intense region = you hit a cilium.
  5. If needed, adjust brightness/contrast to see faint cilia; use the eraser or undo to fix mistakes.
  6. You can download your annotation and share it with us (if you like) or train your own AI model with it.
  7. Load a new image any time.

No experience needed — every contribution helps!
