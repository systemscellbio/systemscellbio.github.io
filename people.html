Philosophy

Our research, teaching, and team culture are guided by a shared set of values.

Respect & Teamwork

We complement each other and succeed together. The biggest advances come from teams tackling ideas from many angles and where everyone has their unique role.

Rigor & Reproducibility

We strive for the highest-quality science that others can trust and build on. We automate procedures to minimize bias and document carefully for full reproducibility.

Open Science

Science is an international team effort. We aim to democratize access to key technologies through open-source, open-access data and tools.

Belonging & Fairness

Our lab shall be a safe, welcoming space where everyone feels valued and has an equal opportunity to contribute, grow, and succeed.

Clear Expectations

We practice open, honest communication and co-develop agreements on goals and expectations, revisited continuously to stay aligned.

Growth & Openness

We practice a culture of empathy, self-reflection, and openness, where feedback flows in both directions and guides continuous refinement.

Teaching & Mentoring

A note from Jan

I believe that teaching and mentoring are among the most rewarding parts of leading a research group. I see mentoring as a dynamic, reciprocal relationship that supports the professional and personal growth of both mentee and mentor. I see feedback as a key to continuous refinement and growth. This means that I am not just trying to help you grow but also want to hear your feedback, to grow myself.

I myself benefited throughout my career from wonderful mentors that trusted in me, that guided me, and helped me grow. Shaped by experience and training across Germany, Norway, Sweden, and the USA, I strive to be a knowledgeable guide and supportive advisor myself, and to lead by example. I trust in individualized support, and aim to adapt my mentoring to each person's goals, needs, and motivations.

A central goal is helping people build independence and ownership. I aim to support mentees to lead their projects, make informed decisions, and grow in confidence.

Jan N. Hansen

Principal Investigator

Jan bridges experimental and computational approaches to tackle open questions in life sciences, combining cell biology, systems biology, technology development, and computer science. He is a passionate advocate for open science and loves mentoring the next generation of researchers in developing an interdisciplinary skill set. Outside the lab, Jan enjoys the outdoors (hiking, SUPing, cross-country skiing), making music on the trumpet and piano, and dancing.
